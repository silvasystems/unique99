    Deck stats

    How your deck score is calculated Your rounded card score average (basic lands excluded) plus a bonus. The bonus adds two parts: one from the average itself (−2 up to +5, highest for averages of 61–80) and one from the category that appears most (Unique Sleepers +3 down to Commander Staples −2).
    How to raise your score Replace Commander Staples and Commander Favorites with Pet Cards or Unique Sleepers that still support your game plan.
